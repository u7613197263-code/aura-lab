Privacy, safety & backup
Watch-only: no wallet connection, signing, buying, selling or withdrawals. Never enter a seed phrase, private key, recovery phrase or exchange secret.
Addresses and snapshots are stored in this browser's IndexedDB without application-level encryption. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile/device may inspect them. The public RPC provider receives the address and request metadata when you refresh.

Backup uses PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM. The passphrase is never stored or transmitted. Losing it makes that backup unrecoverable. Keep the backup file and passphrase separately.
