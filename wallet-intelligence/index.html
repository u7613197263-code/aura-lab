Privacy, safety & backup
This is watch-only. It never asks to connect a wallet, sign a transaction, buy, sell or withdraw. Never enter a seed phrase, private key, recovery phrase or exchange secret.
Wallet addresses and snapshots are stored in this browser's IndexedDB without application-level encryption. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile/device may inspect them. The Solana RPC provider receives the public address and your network metadata when you refresh.

Backup uses PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM in your browser. Losing the passphrase makes that backup unrecoverable. Keep the backup file and passphrase separately.
