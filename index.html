LIMITS & SAFETY

Important before relying on any signal

Meme tokens can lose most or all value, have manipulated liquidity, or become impossible to sell. On-chain diagnostics are partial and do not guarantee safety.

This app uses public endpoints directly from the browser. If a provider blocks browser CORS, rate-limits requests or changes its API, the affected section will show an error. The app does not proxy, scrape private groups or access privileged information.

Paper portfolio and scanner observations are saved in this browser's local storage. They do not sync between devices. Clearing site data, changing browser or opening another profile can remove or separate the ledger.

No seed phrase, wallet connection, signing, automated withdrawal, leverage or real trade execution is included.
